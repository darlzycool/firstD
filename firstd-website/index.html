CREATOR SAFETY

Before you share

Uploads are not enabled in this prototype. Any future user-upload platform should require:

  • Everyone depicted is 18+. Reliably verify age before publication.
  • Documented informed consent from every person shown, including permission to distribute.
  • Ownership or a valid license for every submitted work.
  • Clear, prompt reporting and takedown for illegal or non-consensual content.

This demo does not accept, store, or publish uploads. A real service needs robust age and consent checks plus staffed moderation before launch.

FIRSTD COMMUNITY

Safety, consent & reporting

A responsible user-upload platform puts people first. This prototype is designed around these standards:

  • Adults only: verify age for all creators and everyone depicted; no minors, ever.
  • Explicit consent: documented permission to record and distribute from every person depicted.
  • Respect ownership: uploaders own or have permission to publish submissions.
  • Easy reporting: clear, accessible channels for urgent safety and takedown concerns.
  • Active moderation: trained human review, timely escalation, and transparent decisions.

No uploads, real videos, or moderation backend are connected to this demo.
